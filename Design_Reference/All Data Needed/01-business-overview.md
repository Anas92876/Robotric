# Business Overview

## Who we are

**Robotrick** is a robotics and technology company based in **Aleppo, Syria**, founded in **2022**. We are not just a training center and not just an engineering firm — we're both, plus an educational-institution partner. Three audiences pay us, in three different ways:

1. **Individuals (mainly parents, and youth/adults themselves)** → pay for training programs (robotics, AI, VR, drones, 3D printing, web development, engineering design).
2. **Businesses and individual inventors/engineers** → pay for custom technical projects (robotics, automation, IoT) and 3D printing services.
3. **Schools and educational institutions** → pay us to set up STEM labs and to design/deliver technical curricula, or to provide technical consultation.

Domain: **robotrick.net**. Bilingual site: Arabic (primary, RTL) and English.

## Who buys from us (target customers)

- **Parents of kids age 6+** looking for a serious (not "arts-and-crafts") robotics/tech education for their children.
- **Teens and young adults** (up to university-age) who want deeper, career-track skills: AI, embedded systems, mechatronics, CAD, competitive robotics.
- **University students / young engineers** who need a lab, mentorship, or help finishing a graduation project (electronics, prototyping, technical consultation).
- **Schools and educational institutions** who want to set up a real STEM lab or adopt a robotics/technical curriculum aligned with the Syrian national curriculum.
- **Businesses and individuals** who need a custom robotics/automation/IoT solution built, or 3D printing/prototyping done professionally.

## What makes us different (positioning)

- **Academic rigor, not surface-level "STEM toy" activities** — this is the core differentiator called out in our own founding story: we deliver robotics "the right way, academically."
- **Full-stack capability under one roof**: design → programming → manufacturing → training, so a school or business doesn't need multiple vendors.
- **Proven competition results** — we compete in and place at the National Robotics Olympiad (Syria) and prepare teams for the World Robot Olympiad (WRO). This is real, verifiable credibility (see achievements in file 02).
- **Bilingual, locally rooted** in Aleppo, Syria, with a physical lab/facility (not remote-only).
- **Certification** — students who complete a program and pass a practical assessment get an official Robotrick certificate, verifiable online (see the certificate-verification feature in file 07).

## Business model in one line

Revenue comes from **course/program tuition** (recurring, mostly B2C), **project fees** (one-off, B2B/B2C), **3D printing job fees** (one-off), and **institutional contracts** (STEM lab setup + curriculum design, B2B/B2G, higher-value/lower-frequency). The public website's core commercial job is to turn visitors into one of: a program enrollment, a project/consultation inquiry, a 3D print order, or a school-partnership conversation — see [08-goals-and-success-metrics.md](08-goals-and-success-metrics.md).
