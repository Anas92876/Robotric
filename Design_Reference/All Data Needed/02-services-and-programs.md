## 1. Training Programs

Comprehensive robotics/AI/tech training for all ages, beginner to professional. This is the flagship, highest-volume service.

**Positioning line:** "Transform Your Future with Expert Training" — "Discover our comprehensive training programs in Robotics, AI, VR, 3D Printing, Drones, and Web Development. Designed for all skill levels, from beginners to professionals."

Training is organized into **4 program formats**, and within them, **8 subject "departments"**:

### Program formats

- **☀️ Summer Programs** — intensive holiday programs, learning + fun.
  - *Comprehensive Tech Club for Kids* (6–9 yrs): robotics, visual programming, 3D design/print, educational drone flying, basic electronics, team challenges.
  - *Comprehensive Tech Club for Teens* (10–15 yrs): Python robotics, basic AI, CAD design, engineering mini-projects, teamwork/presentation. *(Was marked "coming soon" on the current site — confirm status with owner.)*

- **❄️ Winter Programs** — run during the school year, apply STEM concepts, prep for competitions.
  - Specialized Robotics Program for Kids (6–8 yrs, ~100 hrs)
  - Microbot Teens / Full Robotics / Specialized Robotics (9–15 yrs) — bridges STEM to engineering, autonomous mission programming, national-olympiad qualification track.
  - Robotics Engineering Camp (12–16 yrs) — CAD design, multi-sensor programming, full project build, present & defend solutions.
  - Youth Engineering Camp (17+) — embedded systems (Arduino/C++), PCB schematic design, professional prototypes, capstone project.

- **📅 Year-Round Programs** — 8 specialized departments, start anytime, self-paced progression through levels. This is the evergreen, always-open enrollment path.

- **🏆 Competition Programs** — World Robot Olympiad (WRO) track, all ages: WRO rules/scoring mastery, autonomous mission robots, robust mechanisms, teamwork under pressure, national/international WRO competition.

### Year-round FAQ (use these verbatim as they answer real buyer objections)

1. **Does my child need prior experience to enroll?** No — beginner tracks exist per department starting from zero; students with background get assessed and placed at the right level.
2. **How many hours per week / what's the schedule?** Usually 2 sessions/week, 1.5–2 hours each, morning or evening, arranged at enrollment.
3. **How long does each level take?** ~12–16 hours per level, ~6–8 weeks at 2 sessions/week; students advance after a practical assessment.
4. **Is there a certificate?** Yes — official Robotrick certificate per completed level (after passing the practical assessment).
5. **What does my child need to bring?** Nothing — all equipment/kits/materials provided. Optional notebook.
6. **Can I try a session before committing?** Yes — free trial session, book via WhatsApp.
