# Robotrick — New Developer Brief

Welcome. You're building the **public website** for Robotrick (robotrick.net) — the marketing/informational site that visitors, parents, schools, and businesses see. You are **not** touching the internal dashboard (student/trainer/reception/admin LMS+CRM system) — that's a separate, existing application and stays as-is.

This folder is your single source of truth for the business. It's written from the *current live site's real content* — extracted directly from the existing codebase — plus notes on what needs your judgment or a decision from Robotrick's owner before you build.

## Read in this order

1. **[01-business-overview.md](01-business-overview.md)** — who Robotrick is, story, mission/vision/values, target customers.
2. **[02-services-and-programs.md](02-services-and-programs.md)** — the full service catalog and every training program, with real descriptions.
3. **[03-brand-identity-and-design.md](03-brand-identity-and-design.md)** — colors, logos, fonts, tone of voice, bilingual (Arabic/English) requirements.
4. **[04-site-structure-and-pages.md](04-site-structure-and-pages.md)** — the sitemap: every page, its purpose, and its real copy.
5. **[05-contact-and-company-info.md](05-contact-and-company-info.md)** — real phone/email/address/social links/hours. Use these verbatim.
6. **[06-content-assets-inventory.md](06-content-assets-inventory.md)** — what real photos/logos/videos already exist and where, vs. what's missing.
7. **[07-technical-integration-notes.md](07-technical-integration-notes.md)** — what the new site needs to talk to (contact form, job applications, certificate verification, blog), SEO/analytics already in place.
8. **[08-goals-and-success-metrics.md](08-goals-and-success-metrics.md)** — what "a good result" means here: how this site is supposed to make money, and what to optimize for.
9. **[09-open-decisions-for-the-owner.md](09-open-decisions-for-the-owner.md)** — real gaps found in the current content (placeholder team bios, inconsistent stats, etc.) that should be resolved with the business owner *before* or *while* you build — don't silently copy these over.

## The one-sentence brief

**Robotrick is a robotics & technology education and engineering-solutions company based in Aleppo, Syria (founded 2022), that trains kids-through-professionals in robotics/AI/VR/drones/3D printing/web dev, builds custom automation & IoT projects for businesses, runs a 3D printing service, and helps schools set up STEM labs and design technical curricula.** The new public site's job is to make all of that legible and trustworthy fast, and turn visitors into leads (contact form / WhatsApp / consultation bookings / program enrollments).

## What "starting from scratch" means here

You are free to choose your own stack, structure, and code — this brief intentionally contains **zero code**, only content and requirements. But the *content* (service names, program details, contact info, achievements, brand colors) should carry over accurately — this is a real business with real customers, not a fresh concept. Where content is placeholder/fake in the current site (see file 09), don't copy it — flag it back to the owner.
